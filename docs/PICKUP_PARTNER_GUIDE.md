# Pickup Partner (Field Executive) Guide

Sign in at `/login` with the account an admin created for you. You'll land on your dashboard —
you cannot see pricing, customer management, or any other admin function; your access is limited
to pickups assigned to you.

## Your workflow

1. **Scheduled Pickups** — lists pickup requests assigned to you, in order of pickup date/time
   slot.
2. Open a pickup to see the customer's address, contact info, and the declared parcel details
   (weight, dimensions) from the original quote.
3. **On arrival — Verify**: weigh the parcel and enter the actual weight. If it differs from
   what was declared, the price automatically recalculates — you'll see the updated amount before
   collecting payment. Always verify before collecting payment, never the other way around.
4. **Collect Payment**: record how the customer paid (cash, UPI, bank transfer) and the amount.
   You cannot collect payment twice on the same request — if you make a mistake, contact an admin
   rather than re-submitting.
5. **Accept**: confirms you're taking the parcel into the shipping network. This is the step that
   actually creates the real order and tracking number — nothing before this point is a trackable
   shipment. Once accepted, the request is complete and moves off your active list.
6. **Reject**: if the customer isn't available, the parcel doesn't match what was declared in a
   way you can't resolve on-site, or any other reason you can't complete the pickup — reject with
   a reason. This does not create an order.

## Status meaning

`ASSIGNED` (you've been assigned, haven't scheduled yet) → `SCHEDULED` (you've confirmed a
pickup time) → `OUT_FOR_PICKUP` (you're en route/on-site) → `VERIFICATION_PENDING` (mid
verify/payment/accept) → `COMPLETED` or `CANCELLED`.

## What you cannot do

Your account has no access to pricing rules, other partners' pickups, customer records outside
your assigned pickups, or any admin/settings screen. If a customer asks you to change the price
outside of a legitimate weight-based recalculation, that's not something your account can do —
route it to an admin.
